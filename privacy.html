Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains how Petalform LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the Closer mobile app ("Closer" or "the app") for iOS and Android. Closer helps you track money you set aside for things you want. Closer does not connect to your bank, and it never holds, moves or invests money. Amounts in the app are numbers you enter yourself.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: an anonymous account identifier created when you start using the app; if you choose to create an account, your email address and the name your sign-in provider shares (Apple, Google or email).To save your wishes to your account, let you sign in on other devices, and restore purchases.
Content you create: wish names, target amounts, savings entries, dates, notes, categories, priorities, product links and photos you add.To provide the app's features. This content is private to your account.
Preferences: currency, reminder settings, appearance.To run reminders and personalise the app.
Subscription status: whether you have Closer Plus and its renewal and expiry dates. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card.To unlock Closer Plus and let you restore it.
Usage analytics (can be turned off in Profile → Privacy & data): which features are used, for example "a wish was created" or "savings were added", with amounts only in broad ranges. We never send wish names, notes, links or exact amounts to analytics.To understand what's working and improve the app.
Crash reports: technical details when the app crashes or hits an unexpected error (device type, OS and app version, error trace). Reports are scrubbed of amounts, notes, names, emails and tokens.To find and fix bugs.

We do not collect your contacts, location, bank information or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Photos and product links

If you add a photo, it is resized on your device and stored privately in your account. You can use the system photo picker without giving Closer access to your whole photo library. If you paste a product link, the app may fetch that public web page directly from your device to suggest a name, price and image; you always confirm or edit the result.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device and only appear if you allow notifications. You can change or turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Service providers

We use these providers to run Closer. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage for your account and content.
  • RevenueCat: manages Closer Plus subscriptions and restores purchases.
  • PostHog: product analytics (only if analytics is on).
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in (if you choose it), in-app purchases and app distribution, under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your data is restricted to your account at the database level, and your sign-in session is stored in your device's secure keychain or keystore. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. Guest accounts that are never used again may be deleted after a period of inactivity. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • Export your data: Profile → Privacy & data → Export my data.
  • Turn off analytics: Profile → Privacy & data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, wishes, photos and savings history. You can also email petalformllc@gmail.com from the address on your account to request deletion.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Closer Plus subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

Closer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
